# Practice questions

Each "## " heading is a stage. Each "- " line under it is one question.

## Screening call
- Walk me through your background in two minutes.
- Why do you want this role?
- What would your last manager say is your biggest weakness?

## Problem solving
- Sign-ups fell 20% last week. How do you find out why?
- Estimate how many coffees a London office of 200 people buys in a year.
- A customer says that we charged them twice. What do you do in the first hour?

## Behavioural
- Tell me about a time that you disagreed with your manager.
- Tell me about a project that failed. What did you learn?
- Tell me about a process that you made faster.
